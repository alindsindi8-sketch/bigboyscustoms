Common questions

Questions & answers

Will it fit my truck?

Give us the year, make, model and the lift or drop you are running. We check offset, backspacing and clearance before you buy.

How does payment work?

Orders placed here are quotes — we call to confirm the size, the finish and the total before anything is charged. Financing is available on qualifying setups.

Can you ship to me?

Yes — wheels, tires and complete packages ship nationwide, packed on pallets with tracking sent to your phone.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are fine seven days a week. For larger suspension jobs a quick call keeps a bay free for your truck.
